## 3. Licensed taxi or NCC: genuine exception, not an improvised drop-off loophole

The municipality's parking FAQ explicitly exempts taxis/NCC performing their service from registration/advance parking booking, allowing free transit for up to **30 minutes after entering the toll gate**. This supports arranging a licensed drop-off/pickup even when private-car parking is sold out. It does not authorize an ordinary rental car to use this exception. Specific early service, return pickup, access operation and total fare must be confirmed with the licensed operator. [Municipal FAQ: NCC/Taxi](https://auronzo.info/parcheggio-tre-cime-di-lavaredo/).

First-party local provider identified: **Dolomiti Driver Service, Mario Larese De Pol**, based in Auronzo di Cadore; the site advertises 24/7/night service and a vehicle for up to nine passengers. It requests a quote and states payment is made when reserving. Contact: **+39 320 2911224**, **info@dolomitidriverservice.it**. No request was sent and no availability or current Tre Cime fare was confirmed. [Provider website and contact form](https://www.dolomitidriverservice.it/).

One other operator publishes Cortina-Rifugio Auronzo at EUR 160 for a car up to two passengers, but explicitly labels its price list **2024**. This is neither a current quote nor a fare from Auronzo/Misurina; do not present it as the travelers' price. [Taxi Cortina Sci published list](https://www.taxicortinasci.com/price-list-taxi-cortina/).

**Assessment:** best candidate to retain arrival by 07:30-08:00 without extra uphill hiking, conditional on a confirmed licensed transfer and price. Ask for both a direct Auronzo-lodging round trip and, if lower legal parking is available, a Misurina round trip; do not assume a shared-shuttle fare.

## 4. Walking from below the gate: permitted, but a substantial extension

The municipality explicitly describes free pedestrian access from Misurina, Lago Antorno and the toll gate along hiking trails to Rifugio Auronzo. It warns that these approaches are genuine mountain hikes, not simple shortcuts. [Current official access guide](https://auronzo.info/tre-cime-di-lavaredo-come-arrivare/).

The Belluno destination authority specifies the shortest listed approach: start **after Lago Antorno, just before the toll gate**, follow **trail 101**, **3 km**, **500 m ascent**, **about 2 hours one way**, difficulty **E**. Despite its title "from Misurina", these measurements do not start at Lake Misurina itself. [Official route description](https://www.visitdolomitibellunesi.com/it/pois/al-rifugio-auronzo-da-misurina). The municipal hiking map's route 1 repeats these values; both map and route-table pages were rendered and visually checked. [Municipal hiking map](https://www.comune.auronzo.bl.it/s3prod/uploads/ckeditor/attachments/9/4/1/5/0/TREKKING_AURONZO_MISURINA.pdf).

Planning estimates, not measured live routes: allow roughly **2-2.5 hours uphill from a legal Antorno parking space**, including the approach to trail 101; roughly **2.5-3 hours from the Misurina lake/parking area**, depending on the exact space, route and pace. The official access guide places the gate around 500 m beyond Antorno and around 3 km from Misurina. Allow about **1.5-2 hours to descend the trail**, plus the walk back to the actual car park; this downhill allowance is our conservative estimate, not a separate published timed route. [Access geography](https://auronzo.info/tre-cime-di-lavaredo-come-arrivare/).

The already-planned roughly 10 km / 4-5 hour Tre Cime loop starts at Rifugio Auronzo. Walking up and down from Antorno adds roughly 7-8 km including approaches and several hours: budget about **17-18 km and 8-10 hours overall with breaks**, depending on parking and pace. These are derived planning estimates, not a verified GPX total. Starting at Misurina adds further distance. Do not silently add this climb to the existing moderate day or stack Cadini onto it. No route conditions/weather were verified in this research.

## 5. Where parking is and is not an acceptable fallback

**The upper access road is not an overflow parking area.** Article 2 of the currently linked municipal parking regulation specifies a stopping/parking prohibition with forced removal along the access. The regulation defines the road as Prati di Rimbianco to Rifugio Auronzo. [Current municipal regulation, articles 1-2](https://auronzo.info/parcheggio-tre-cime-di-lavaredo/regolamento/). Before the barrier, use an actual authorized space and comply with the posted duration, vehicle category and payment conditions; an empty roadside verge is not evidence of permission.

There are paid municipal car-parking areas at Misurina, but no particular empty all-day space has been verified. The municipality's publicly linked parking table is from an older attachment (2021), so its old daily/hourly tariffs were **not** promoted as current 2026 prices. [Municipal parking page](https://comune.auronzo.bl.it/it/page/parcheggi-comunali).

The user specifically requested traveler reports. A 2026 Reddit discussion reports unrestricted parking on the Chalet side of Lago Antorno and **two-hour disc parking on the other side**. This is an **unverified community report**, not municipal permission, not a guaranteed free space, and not evidence that private hotel parking can be used by non-guests. Confirm the actual sign on arrival. [Reported Antorno parking distinction](https://www.reddit.com/r/TreCime/comments/1w2gfb3/sunrise_hike_to_cadini_di_misurina_viewpoint/). Another 2026 discussion warns of fines for exceeding two-hour parking limits; self-claimed official employment by its author was not independently verified. [Traveler/community discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/TreCime/comments/1uoypxp/helpful_information/).

Older walking descriptions mention isolated spaces by the toll gate and parking used for Fonda Savio approaches. These are leads only; no current all-day authorization or capacity was verified, so they are not recommended as assured parking. [2023 first-party route description](https://www.hiwio.com/it/Articolo/Escursione-al-Rifugio-Auronzo-di-Misurina-1481).

## Recommendation for this trip, not an itinerary change

First choice if a later start is acceptable: legal all-day parking in Misurina and line 50, or board its direct service from Auronzo to avoid the lower parking search. If preserving the original early start is important, seek a quote from a licensed NCC/taxi with confirmed return arrangements. Treat Antorno plus walking as a deliberately longer hiking day, conditional on legal all-day parking and suitable conditions, not a minor parking workaround. Do not rely on roadside parking, old "arrive before the gate opens" advice, an unconfirmed lift, or a two-hour parking space for an all-day hike. No booking or schedule change was made.
